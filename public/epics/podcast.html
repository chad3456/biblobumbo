Samvada

Four conversations on Rama and Krishna, read from Valmiki's Ramayana, the Mahabharata and the Bhagavad Gita. Each turn shows its source and a painting of the moment being discussed.

Sources: Valmiki Ramayana (Bala, Ayodhya, Aranya, Kishkindha, Yuddha and Uttara Kandas), the Mahabharata (Adi, Sabha, Vana, Udyoga, Bhishma, Drona, Karna, Shalya, Stri, Shanti, Ashvamedhika and Mausala Parvas) and the Bhagavad Gita. References follow the common numbering; chapter and verse numbers vary between editions. English renderings are close paraphrases. The voices are your device's own speech voices, and the drone is synthesised in the browser.
